# Contributor Ladder

Roles within VirtFoundry. Advance by sustained participation. All participants follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Contributor

Anyone who improves the project (issues, PRs, docs, testing, discussions).

- Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and Conventional Commits
- Privileges: public contribution; eligible for review help from maintainers

## Reviewer

Trusted contributors asked to review PRs in an area they know (API, UI, operator, charts).

- Requirements: multiple merged PRs; constructive reviews; understanding of CI
- Privileges: informal review requests; path to maintainer nomination

## Maintainer

Write access; merge authority; project stewardship. Listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

- Requirements: see [GOVERNANCE.md — Becoming a Maintainer](GOVERNANCE.md#becoming-a-maintainer)
- Privileges: merge, releases, roadmap input, security response (as assigned)

## Inactivity

Maintainers inactive for **12 months** may move to emeritus (GOVERNANCE / MAINTAINERS). Contributors may be asked to re-engage before nomination.
